# Reporting Rules

Registers are operational working state. Reports are communication outputs. Manage the project in the registers; communicate it through reports.

Use references/presentation-rules.md for the three information layers, default response order, progressive disclosure, and translation of internal records into human language.

## PM-Facing Report Contract

A normal report starts with:

1. Result or overall condition
2. What needs attention
3. Decision required, if any
4. Important risks or gaps
5. Next action

Keep domain detail, source identifiers, process mechanics, and evidence records available through links or on-demand detail. Do not make the reader decode internal identifiers.


## Flow

```text
Registers and external sources -> Processing -> Report
```

The agent generates report content from the current registers and relevant external sources. A report never becomes a source of truth and is never the place where facts are maintained.

## Report Principles

1. Every claim cites its register item or source section. Missing evidence is stated, not assumed.
2. Reports lead with the overall condition and the most important finding in the first 3 lines.
3. Revisions report only what changed since the last report. Unchanged content is referenced, not repeated.
4. Empty sections are omitted.
5. Registers are linked or referenced; their content is not reproduced in full.
6. Every produced report carries the attribution label directly under its title:

```text
**AI-generated by:** Alson (A.I Agent) on behalf of Agno JF
```

7. Conditions follow `definitions.md` (Healthy, Watch, Action required, Insufficient evidence). Trends use Improving, Stable, Worsening, Unknown.
8. Forecasts and trends need at least two data points. Confidence is High (direct measurement), Medium (derived), or Low (assumption).
9. A dimension with Low confidence evidence defaults to Watch unless High confidence evidence says otherwise.

## Evidence Rules

1. Evidence must be current: within the reporting period or labeled "as of [date]".
2. Missing evidence produces Insufficient evidence, never Healthy.
3. Facts, inferences, and PMI-based assessment views are labeled separately.
4. Conflicts are reported with both citations.

## Output Locations

| Output | Location |
|---|---|
| Status report | `<control-root>/status-reports/<slug>.md` |
| Governance update or escalation | `<control-root>/governance-updates/<slug>.md` |
| Summary or briefing | `<control-root>/summaries/<slug>.md` |

Slug format: `YYYY-MM-DD-<short-topic>`. Never overwrite a prior output file.

## Report Audience

Match the report to the reader. A sponsor briefing leads with condition, decisions, and escalation. A team status leads with progress, actions, and blockers. The status report instruction defines the default structure; tailor to audience per project preferences in `project-control.md`.
