# Project Situation Assessment

## Overall Position

**Position:** At risk
**Confidence:** Medium
**Basis:** Objective, scope, and governance are clear and well documented (intake accepted 100%; decision makers named for all 8 decisions). But the platform decision (DEC-01) blocks all build work and no build plan exists, while the ~1 month deadline is fixed and one unproven assumption (AI-assisted build) carries the largest timeline risk. Delivery, schedule, and resources score below 3; nothing is broken yet, but delay compounds with each day.
